The Red Crystal Shrimp, or CRS, is the crown jewel of the freshwater shrimp hobby. Originating from selective breeding of the Bee Shrimp, these invertebrates require pristine water conditions and specialized substrate to maintain their vibrant red and white coloration.

  • verified Aged water with low carbonate hardness (KH 0-1) is essential for successful molting.
  • verified Highly sensitive to copper and nitrates; we recommend a well-established planted tank.
